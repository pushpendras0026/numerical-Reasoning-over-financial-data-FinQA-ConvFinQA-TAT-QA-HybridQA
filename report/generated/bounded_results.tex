Seed 2626 and all three packing methods are fixed; no selected examples overlap the earlier pilots. HybridQA is unevaluated: the genuine linked evidence corpus remains missing.

Subsets and rare successes yield unstable intervals and cannot establish population improvement. Zero-width bootstrap intervals for all-zero samples reflect absent empirical variation and do not establish equivalence. Related datasets share source tables, so scores and uncertainty are kept separate. FinQA dependency packing achieved two correct executions out of 883, compared with none for ordinary and length matched; this is weak evidence rather than guaranteed improvement. No changes to prompts or outputs were tuned against evaluation answers.
